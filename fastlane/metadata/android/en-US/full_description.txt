A deliberately tiny, ad-free and tracker-free Android home-screen widget showing the Bitcoin price in EUR and USD.

<b>Features</b>

- EUR and USD price, 24h change, 24h high/low and last update time
- Manual refresh plus automatic background refresh (about every 15-30 minutes, subject to Android's battery scheduling)
- Tap the widget to open a focused chart screen: 10 minutes, 1 hour, 24 hours, 4 days and all time, with touch-to-inspect values
- Choose to display EUR, USD or both - in the app and the widget
- Bilingual interface (English and German)
- No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no unnecessary permissions

<b>Data sources</b>

Price data comes from the public CoinGecko market endpoint with a public Coinbase fallback. Historical chart data uses Blockchain.com and Frankfurter. No API key or exchange account is needed.

<b>Open source</b>

The full source code is available under the MIT license. If you like the app, you can support it with an optional donation (link in the app footer) - the app is and stays free, without ads.
